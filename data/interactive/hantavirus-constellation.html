What SignalWatch has learned

SHAPE · The hantavirus discussion is four emerging narratives spread thin across 10 platforms — 107 scored posts in all. It is low-heat: anger, contempt, paranoia and grievance carry it, while the violence dimension stays near zero. Nothing here reads as an active threat.

HOT POCKETS · The heat concentrates in two places — the "‘Hanta’ = fake/scam" framing on Rumble (peak VLH 36.5, contempt-led) and the Plandemic-successor cluster on Bitchute (peak 29, anger + hate + paranoia). Those are the pockets to keep a sensor on.

MOBILIZATION · Only one node shows call-to-action rhetoric: the Baba Vanga 2026 prophecy on Bitchute. It's an indicator, not a verdict — a single planning-flavored pocket, not a campaign.

IDENTITY GAP · No accounts were attributable for the scored atoms, so each node is a narrative's posts on one platform, not a named spreader. That thin identity signal is itself a finding: the conversation is diffuse and low-attribution, not driven by a visible coordinated set of actors.
