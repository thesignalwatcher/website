Pseudoscience

Junk science tracked apart from conspiracy theories — claims that are wrong about physical reality, history, or how the world works, rather than hidden-villain plots. 0 theories, updated 2026-06-28.

What makes this a separate shelf: these are flagged by SignalWatch's pseudoscience scanner (the A2 marker battery — physical-law violations, fringe-cosmology, pseudo-archaeology) and are not primarily conspiratorial. A theory with a central malevolent actor causing or covering up harm (chemtrails spraying, anti-vax, directed-energy attacks, suppressed-cure plots) stays a conspiracy theory, even when it misuses science. The cut here is: the wrong claim is the point, not a villain.
