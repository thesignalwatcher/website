Voice of Reason

There is no kernel of verified fact here — no confirmed case, no peer-reviewed measurement, no whistleblower record. The claim rests entirely on a method: the idea that shoulder width, hip ratio, or skull shape in a photograph can reliably reveal a person's sex assigned at birth. That method has never been validated. Human body proportions overlap enormously between sexes; lighting, camera angle, lens distortion, posture, clothing, and image compression all shift the ratios that 'transvestigation' treats as fixed signals. When a technique produces a 'hit' regardless of who is analyzed, it is not detecting anything real — it is finding what it was designed to find.

What makes this feel compelling is a powerful fear — that trusted public figures are not what they appear — combined with the assumption that someone powerful arranged it. Once that assumption is in place, every ambiguous jawline or broad shoulder seems to confirm it, and the absence of documentary evidence becomes proof the cover-up is working. But a framework that cannot, even in principle, be shown wrong by evidence is not revealing a hidden truth. The simpler question worth sitting with: if the method works, why does it identify nearly every woman it is applied to?
