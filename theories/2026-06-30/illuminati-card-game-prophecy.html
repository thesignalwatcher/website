That context matters. A game built around secret societies, shadowy power, and chaos was always going to feature ominous towers, political figures, and occult imagery. That is the genre, not a leak.

The fear is real — that powerful people script history — and once that assumption is in place, ambiguous artwork seems to fall into place around it. But the feeling of a pattern is not the same as evidence of one. For this claim to hold, you'd need every vague card to count as a hit and every miss to count as nothing.
