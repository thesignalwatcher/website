Voice of Reason

The doubling sequence 1-2-4-8-7-5 is a real pattern — it's what you get when you repeatedly double a number and reduce each result to a single digit, a standard technique in modular arithmetic. That's genuine mathematics. And toroidal coil designs do have real engineering uses; wound coils are common in electronics. So there's a real starting point here.

The leap comes when a visually interesting numerical pattern gets promoted to a hidden key to reality. Patterns that repeat in modular arithmetic aren't mysterious — they're a predictable property of how numbers behave in a fixed base. Calling them a cosmic code adds a layer the math itself doesn't supply. The over-unity energy claim carries serious weight: it would require revising conservation of energy, one of the most tested principles in physics. Researchers who have built Rodin-pattern coils report no anomalous output — the results match standard electromagnetic expectations.

The framework feels compelling partly because it treats the absence of mainstream adoption as proof of suppression. But if every missing confirmation becomes evidence of a cover-up, no test can ever count against the idea. A simpler question worth sitting with: what would a verified, replicated measurement of over-unity actually look like, and has anyone produced one?
