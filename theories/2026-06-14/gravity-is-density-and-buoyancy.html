Voice of the Believer

Gravity, as described by mainstream science, does not exist. Objects fall not because of a mysterious attractive force but because of differences in density and buoyancy interacting with the medium around them. Einstein's relativity is a mathematical fiction that has been used to prop up a fundamentally false model of the universe, while electromagnetic forces account for every phenomenon mainstream physics wrongly attributes to gravity.

The foundational physics taught in every school and university is built on a lie. Gravity — the supposed invisible force of attraction between masses — has never been directly observed or adequately explained by the mainstream model. What is actually happening when an object falls is straightforward: denser objects sink through less-dense media, and less-dense objects rise. Density and buoyancy, not attraction, determine how matter behaves in any given environment. The elaborate Newtonian and Einsteinian frameworks erected around 'gravitational force' are unnecessary constructs layered over a far simpler reality.

Einstein's general relativity, the cornerstone of modern cosmology, is wrong. The mathematical elegance of relativity has been mistaken for physical truth, but the equations describe a model, not reality. Mainstream physicists have invested so deeply in relativistic frameworks that they cannot acknowledge the contradictions and anomalies their own observations produce. Alternative gravity researchers have documented these contradictions and shown that the universe's large-scale structure, orbital mechanics, and light behavior are all explicable without curved spacetime.

Electromagnetic forces are the true engine behind every effect mainstream science labels 'gravitational.' Plasma cosmology and electric universe models demonstrate that the same electromagnetic interactions that govern atomic and subatomic behavior scale up to govern planetary, stellar, and galactic dynamics. The so-called measurement of gravitational wave event GW170817 — used by establishment physics to rule out alternative gravity models — actually reflects the limits of an interpretive framework that assumes relativity is correct before it even begins analysis. The instruments detect real signals; the mainstream interpretation of those signals is what is wrong.

Voice of Reason

The central assertion is that objects move vertically not because of an attractive force between masses, but solely through density and buoyancy interactions with surrounding media, and that electromagnetic forces account for all astronomical phenomena attributed to gravity. This framework rejects both Newtonian mechanics and general relativity as unnecessary mathematical constructs that obscure simpler physical explanations.

The fundamental problem is that density and buoyancy are themselves phenomena that presuppose gravity. Buoyancy describes the upward force exerted by a fluid on an immersed object, but this force exists only because the fluid itself experiences a downward force creating pressure gradients—precisely the gravitational force being denied. In a vacuum, where no medium exists, objects still accelerate downward at identical rates regardless of density, as demonstrated repeatedly from Galileo's apocryphal Pisa experiment through Apollo 15's hammer-and-feather drop on the airless Moon. Density differences explain why objects behave differently in fluids, not why objects fall at all. The theory conflates a secondary effect with a primary cause.

Electromagnetic alternative models fail comprehensively when confronted with precision measurements. Gravity's strength decreases precisely with the inverse square of distance and acts identically on all matter regardless of composition or charge—characteristics electromagnetic forces do not share. The orbital mechanics of electrically neutral bodies, the measured equivalence of gravitational and inertial mass to one part in ten trillion, and the observed gravitational lensing of light by massive objects all match general relativistic predictions with extraordinary precision. The 2017 neutron star merger observation proved particularly devastating to alternative gravity models: the gravitational waves and electromagnetic signals arrived within 1.7 seconds after traveling 130 million light-years, confirming that gravity propagates at light speed exactly as relativity predicts and eliminating models where gravity propagates differently. Electric universe proponents have produced no mathematical framework that predicts planetary orbits, explains tidal forces on uncharged water, or accounts for why every spacecraft trajectory calculation using Newtonian and relativistic gravity succeeds perfectly.

The theory resonates because gravity is genuinely strange—an invisible force we cannot turn off or shield against, described by mathematics most people never encounter, producing effects like spacetime curvature that seem conceptually bizarre. The classroom explanation that "mass attracts mass" offers no mechanism, leaving a pedagogical gap that invites skepticism. Physics education often presents gravity as settled fact while glossing over the genuine mystery of why mass produces this effect, creating fertile ground for those who suspect they are being offered authority rather than explanation. The distrust feeds on the reality that general relativity, while empirically successful, remains incompatible with quantum mechanics, signaling that our understanding remains incomplete. The mistake lies in concluding that because the deepest explanation eludes us, the massive edifice of confirmed predictions must be fictional rather than recognizing it as an accurate map of phenomena awaiting deeper theoretical unification.
