Voice of the Believer

The Nazi regime operated esoteric scientific programs that produced functional UFO-style electro-propulsive aircraft, the true physics behind which has been suppressed by a global elite that controls banking, industry, and government. After World War II, this technology was brought to New Mexico, where Dr. Lincoln La Paz was employed by the U.S. government to cover up its origins. The Nazi regime's scientific programs went far beyond the conventional rockets and jets presented in mainstream accounts. At their core were esoteric research programs aimed at developing electro-propulsive aircraft — craft that operated on principles of ether physics rather than conventional aerodynamics or chemical propulsion. Josef Goebbels' public references to Wunderwaffen were a surface-level acknowledgment of a deeper reality: that German scientists, potentially in collaboration with figures such as Nikola Tesla and drawing on frameworks like Walter Russell's Spiral Octave Periodic Table, were working with physics that the establishment has since buried. The so-called Nazi Bell project is a key artifact of this suppressed history, pointing toward breakthroughs that the victorious powers immediately moved to conceal.

When the war ended, the technology did not disappear — it was transferred. New Mexico became a focal point for this absorbed knowledge, and Dr. Lincoln La Paz, employed by the U.S. government, was specifically tasked with helping cover up the presence and origins of Nazi flying saucer technology in the region. What the public was told about UFO sightings, foreign aircraft, and exotic propulsion was carefully managed disinformation, designed to make real electro-propulsion seem like fantasy while the elite quietly operationalized it.

The suppression runs deeper than any single government program. The word 'occult' simply means 'hidden' or 'secret,' and a cult is any group that uses secret teachings reserved for initiates. By that definition, an entire domain of physics has been rendered occult — declared off-limits to the public and to independent researchers. The real science of electro-propulsion has been replaced with deliberately false physics, promoted so thoroughly through academic and media channels that virtually no one outside the esoteric elite knows the truth. The high priests of this occult ether physics are the elite owners of corporate banking, industrial, and mineral interests, who selectively admit certain scientists, military officers, and government officials into the secret teachings that underpin their control of energy and propulsion technology.

The result is a conspiracy of giant proportions: the public remains in enforced ignorance, fed propaganda that obliterates any trail leading to the truth about ether physics and electro-propulsion. The technology itself has been classified not for reasons of national security in any conventional sense, but to preserve the monopoly of a global elite over the most transformative energy and propulsion science ever developed. What is dismissed as myth or wartime propaganda — the wonder weapons — is, in fact, the cover story for something that worked, and that continues to be used by those who seized it.

Voice of Reason

The central assertion is that Nazi Germany developed working aircraft based on exotic "ether physics" and electro-propulsion principles fundamentally different from conventional aerodynamics, that this technology was captured and brought to the American Southwest after the war, and that both the physics underlying it and the technology itself have been deliberately suppressed by a global elite who monopolize transformative energy science while feeding the public falsified physics through academic channels.

The documentary record contradicts nearly every element of this narrative. Nazi Germany did pursue advanced weapons programs—the V-2 rocket, jet aircraft, and various experimental designs—but these operated on conventional physics and were thoroughly documented by Allied technical intelligence teams who examined facilities, interrogated scientists, and published detailed reports. The mythical "Nazi Bell" derives almost entirely from a single 2000 book by Igor Witkowski, based on alleged testimony from a Polish source who claimed to have seen SS documents that have never been produced or independently verified. No physical evidence of the Bell exists, no contemporary Nazi documents reference it, and the site in Poland identified as its test location shows no unusual radiation or physical traces. The technology supposedly brought to New Mexico would have left extensive documentation in military procurement, testing facilities, personnel assignments, and budget allocations—none of which exists. Dr. Lincoln La Paz was a real astronomer who studied meteorites and green fireballs in New Mexico, but his published work and archival papers show him doing exactly what he claimed: investigating atmospheric phenomena and meteor trajectories, not managing a technology cover-up.

The methodological problems run deeper than absent evidence. The theory treats the absence of documentation as proof of suppression rather than evidence of non-existence, making it unfalsifiable. It misunderstands how physics works: the abandonment of luminiferous ether theory followed the Michelson-Morley experiment and the success of special relativity in making testable predictions—not elite conspiracy. Walter Russell was a sculptor and self-taught mystic whose periodic table has no predictive power and contradicts observed atomic behavior. The theory conflates wartime propaganda about wonder weapons with actual capability, ignoring that Goebbels' claims were designed to sustain German morale as conventional defeat loomed. Perhaps most tellingly, electro-propulsion would require power sources and engineering materials that would themselves constitute revolutionary breakthroughs, yet believers never explain where those came from.

The theory resonates because it builds on genuine historical facts: Operation Paperclip did bring German scientists to America, some Nazi research was classified, and the early Cold War government did lie about various military programs. The post-war military-industrial complex did maintain extensive secrecy, and major energy technologies are indeed controlled by powerful corporate interests. These kernels of truth provide plausibility scaffolding for a narrative that transforms understandable frustration with energy monopolies and government secrecy into a totalizing explanation where all of modern physics becomes a lie and technological liberation remains forever just out of reach, stolen by shadowy elites.
