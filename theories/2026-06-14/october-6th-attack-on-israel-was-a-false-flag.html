The central assertion is that Israeli forces orchestrated the October 7, 2023 attack themselves, conducted most of the killings that day, and used the event as premeditated justification for military operations against Iran and its regional allies. The evidentiary problems are overwhelming. The attack was extensively documented in real-time by the attackers themselves, who wore body cameras, livestreamed killings on social media, and celebrated their actions publicly. Hamas officially claimed responsibility and released propaganda videos featuring the assault.
