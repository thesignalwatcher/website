Core claims

  • Hospitals and doctors deliberately killed COVID-19 patients by placing them on ventilators in order to collect enhanced federal (CARES Act) payments — medical murder for profit.
  • An official report / Northwestern University study proves ventilators killed 'nearly all' COVID-19 patients who died early in the pandemic.
  • 80-88% of people put on ventilators died, which proves ventilators caused the deaths.
  • Remdesivir was a deliberate euthanasia agent built into the standard hospital COVID protocol to kill patients (the 'Run - Death Is Near' claim).

  • COVID-19 deaths were an illusion — patients were poisoned to death with ill-advised protocols to fake the severity of the pandemic and drive vaccine uptake.

Explanation of Belief

This theory asserts that hospitals and physicians deliberately placed COVID-19 patients on ventilators not out of medical necessity but to collect enhanced CARES Act federal reimbursements, thereby causing or accelerating patient deaths, and that the antiviral drug remdesivir was similarly deployed as a de facto euthanasia agent to inflate COVID-19 mortality figures and manufacture justification for mass vaccination campaigns.

Origins: The theory emerged in mid-to-late 2020, accelerating after New York emergency physician Dr. Cameron Kyle-Sidell posted videos questioning early ventilator protocols, and was subsequently amplified by figures including Dr. Bryan Ardis, who popularized the 'Run-Death Is Near' remdesivir framing; it spread widely through alternative health platforms, social media networks, and podcasts hostile to mainstream pandemic policy, drawing selectively on real clinical debates about early aggressive ventilator use and actual CARES Act reimbursement differentials.

How believers defend it: Adherents treat fact-checks, platform removals, and official medical rebuttals as confirmatory evidence of an institutional cover-up designed to protect financial and pharmaceutical interests, and they reframe the genuine early-pandemic clinical uncertainty around ventilator protocols—as well as documented high mortality rates among ventilated patients—as suppressed proof that medical authorities knowingly continued lethal practices rather than as evidence that the sickest patients were those most likely to require mechanical ventilation.

Voice of Reason

Early in the pandemic, clinicians genuinely debated when to intubate COVID patients, and mortality among the most critically ill ventilated patients was strikingly high — sometimes 80% or more in early New York data. Some physicians, including Dr. Kyle-Sidell, publicly questioned whether standard ventilator settings fit COVID's unusual lung behavior. These are real events, and the questions they raised were legitimate and debated openly in medical literature. CARES Act funding did provide higher payments for COVID-classified and ventilated patients, a fact that is publicly documented.

The leap happens when high mortality among the sickest patients — people already near death — gets read as proof the treatment caused the deaths, and when a payment structure gets re-read as a motive for murder. Both moves ignore the simpler read: very sick people die at high rates, and funding categories follow severity, not the other way around. Once you assume a hidden coordinated kill protocol, every ambiguous detail — a drug's side effects, a high mortality rate, a payment code — seems to confirm it, because the framework has no room for disconfirming evidence. The plainer question worth sitting with: if hospitals were deliberately killing patients for revenue, what would that evidence actually look like, and where is it?

Origins & History

The genuinely high ICU mortality among early ventilated NYC COVID patients, combined with the real and rapidly-shifting intubation-timing debate, the real CARES Act 20% payment add-on, and real remdesivir adverse events in the critically ill.

First surfaced: Spring 2020. Three near-simultaneous seeds in New York-centered discourse: emergency physician Cameron Kyle-Sidell's March 31, 2020 video questioning ventilator use in COVID; the widely reported ~88% ventilated-patient mortality figure from an April 2020 NYC analysis; and Scott Jensen's April 8-9, 2020 Fox News ('Ingraham Angle') interview introducing the $13k/$39k payment framing. A viral April 30, 2020 video alleging NYC hospitals were 'murdering' patients with ventilators fused these into an intent narrative. The 'ventilators killed nearly ALL' revival came via The People's Voice on May 13, 2023.

Originators: No single originator. The clinical seed was a legitimate physician debate (Kyle-Sidell) that was distorted; the financial seed was Scott Jensen's interview; the mature 'deliberate kill protocol' synthesis was crystallized in 2021-2022 by Bryan Ardis (with attorney Thomas Renz supplying figures) and propagated through InfoWars, the Stew Peters Network, and the Reiner Fuellmich 'Grand Jury' event.

How it spread: Fox News (Ingraham) -> WorldNetDaily / The Spectator / X22 Report -> Facebook memes (2020); Kyle-Sidell's video -> Time / STAT coverage -> conspiracist reframing; Ardis -> InfoWars, Stew Peters, Rumble, BitChute, Brighteon, Global Research, Telegram anti-vaccine channels (2021-2022), including Australia-based channels tied to anti-lockdown protests; Elon Musk and Joe Rogan mass-amplification; The People's Voice 2023 headline -> Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, and French-language reshares.
