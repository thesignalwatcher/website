There is a real and legitimate tradition of scientists questioning specific claims about particular pathogens — that kind of skepticism is how science is supposed to work. The problem here is a much larger leap: from "some findings deserve scrutiny" to "no virus has ever caused any disease, and everyone who says otherwise is lying."

The leap happens when ambiguous methodological debates get reread as proof of a global cover-up. Once you accept that assumption, every piece of contrary evidence — the decades of independent researchers across competing countries and institutions producing consistent results — becomes "part of the conspiracy" rather than evidence against it.
