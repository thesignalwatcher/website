The specific claims about Allen's hotel room and security "stand-down" collapse immediately against the documented timeline. According to the affidavit, Allen made a reservation on April 6 for three nights at the Washington Hilton, the site of the annual press gala. The conspiracy theory attributes this to "deliberate facilitation" by deep state operatives; the affidavit attributes it to Allen planning his own attack weeks in advance. His room was on the tenth floor of a large hotel — not "directly above the event space," which was the ground-floor ballroom. Magnetometers functioning and agents returning fire are not the operational signature of a stand-down order.
