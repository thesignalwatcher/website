Voice of Reason

The collapses of the Twin Towers did produce enormous quantities of fine dust and pulverized concrete — that part is real and documented. Engineers attribute it to the mechanics of progressive collapse: when floors pancake at high speed under enormous kinetic energy, concrete decking is ground between steel members and ejected outward as powder. The fires, fed by tens of thousands of gallons of jet fuel and office contents, also contributed to the destruction of non-structural material. Those same engineers note that steel framing did not disappear — recovery crews removed roughly 185,000 tons of steel from Ground Zero over months.

The leap here is reading genuine, striking dust as proof of a secret weapon rather than as a predictable output of a high-energy structural failure. Once a hidden machine is assumed, every ambiguous detail — an oddly scorched car, a nearby hurricane, a circular hole in glass — seems to fall into place, and that feeling of fit can be mistaken for evidence. But a framework that treats any missing proof as further proof of concealment cannot be tested or corrected. The simpler question worth sitting with: if 185,000 tons of steel were carted away over months, were the towers really "dustified"?
