Voice of Reason

Start with what's undeniable: the collapse of two towers was one of the most-documented events in history, captured by dozens of independent cameras — news helicopters, tourists, residents, and journalists — from every angle, simultaneously. Hundreds of on-the-ground witnesses reported seeing a large commercial aircraft strike each tower. Flight manifests, radar tracks, recovered wreckage, and passenger DNA all exist in public records.

The theory asks you to accept one enormous leap: that every one of those independent feeds was intercepted and composited in real time, that hundreds of eyewitnesses were either wrong or paid to lie, and that physical wreckage was planted. Notice the structure — once you grant a hidden machine that large, no absence of evidence can count against it, because the machine would have removed it. That feeling of pieces clicking into place is what happens when an explanation is built to be unfalsifiable, not when it's true. The aluminum-versus-steel objection sounds technical but ignores the role of mass and velocity in penetration physics. A simpler question worth sitting with: what would the actual evidence need to look like for this theory to be wrong?

Origins & History

The saturation live-broadcast coverage of the second impact itself — the very ubiquity of the footage became the target — plus the real scarcity of first-strike video.

First surfaced: No-plane/alternative-impact claims trace to Thierry Meyssan's early post-9/11 work (2002); the CGI/TV-fakery form crystallized around 2006-2008 with the 'September Clues' video and Ace Baker's 'The Key,' and Morgan Reynolds' public advocacy in the mid-2000s.

Originators: Thierry Meyssan (early alternative-impact framing); the TV-fakery variant popularized by the anonymous 'September Clues' producer and 'Ace Baker'; hologram variant associated with David Shayler and Morgan Reynolds.

How it spread: Early conspiracy books/sites -> 'September Clues' viral video and frame-by-frame YouTube analysis culture -> intense intra-movement fight leading to forum bans -> residual persistence in fringe channels and periodic doctored-video revivals (e.g., 2022), now feeding into deepfake-era 'you can't trust any footage' framing.
